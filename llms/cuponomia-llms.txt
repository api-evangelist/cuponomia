# Cuponomia

> Cuponomia is a Brazilian coupons and cashback platform that helps consumers save money on online purchases from more than 2,000 partner retailers. A pioneer in popularizing free discount coupons in Brazil, the company expanded into cashback in 2019 and now operates a website, a browser extension, and mobile apps alongside a Prime membership tier. It is backed by 500 Global.
